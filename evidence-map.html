How to interpret the score

  • 0-7: clarify before selling. Route to SCORE, ROUTE, PROFILE, or VISIBILITY.
  • 8-12: evidence gap. Route to PROOF, Trust Route Checklist, or this Buyer Evidence Map.
  • 13-16: conversion-route gap. Route to GROWTH, PROFILE, SPRINT, or KIT depending on fit.
  • 17-20: ready for scope conversation. Route to SPRINT, GROWTH, or SYSTEM only after fit, scope, no-guarantee boundary, and explicit buyer intent.

Micro-response format

  2. Evidence gap.
  3. First safe fix.

  6. No-guarantee boundary.

Safe first reply

This sounds like an evidence-route issue: buyer problem, proof, process, and next step need to line up before pitching harder.

Quick fit-check: is the biggest gap your profile, offer page, proof/examples, or follow-up path?

Operating boundary

This is a diagnostic and routing asset. It is not a guarantee of leads, replies, sales, revenue, followers, rankings, AI recommendations, or conversion lift. Do not record a CRM lead unless a reply, comment, DM, or site inquiry is tool-confirmed or owner-confirmed.
