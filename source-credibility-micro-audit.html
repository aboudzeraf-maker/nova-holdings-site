Source Credibility Micro-Audit Template

A compact diagnostic for confirmed proof, evidence, trust-gap, and AI-visibility inquiries. It maps one claim to one proof object, one verification path, one constraint, and one safe next route before any product-specific pitch.

No fake proofNo payment-first languageNo guaranteed leads or rankingsOne route first

Use only after

  • Tool-confirmed X reply, mention, or comment.
  • Confirmed LinkedIn comment/DM.
  • Confirmed owned-site inquiry.
  • Owner-provided inbound message.

Do not count

  • Views or impressions.
  • Market-listening results.
  • Owned pages alone.
  • Assumed visitors or intent.

Best fit

Use when the buyer asks what proves a claim, whether a source is credible, what evidence should be shown, or whether buyers/AI-assisted discovery can understand the company.

Inputs required

1. Exact claim needing trust.
2. Buyer question or doubt.
3. Existing proof object.
4. Source location.
5. What the proof does not prove.
6. Safest next step.

If an input is missing, ask one clarification question before scoring.
