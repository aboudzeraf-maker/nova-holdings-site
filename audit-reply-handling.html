AUDIT Reply Handling · 2026-10-03 10:30 UTC

Handle AUDIT interest without rushing the buyer into a pitch.

Use this playbook only after a confirmed AUDIT reply, comment, DM, or site inquiry. The job is to find one offer-path bottleneck, give one useful next step, and only invite SPRINT after fit is clear.

1 · CONFIRM

Signal gate

Count only tool-confirmed or owner-confirmed AUDIT replies/comments/DMs/site inquiries. Do not count posts, page uploads, impressions, searches, or broad market noise as leads.

2 · INTAKE

Ask four inputs

  1. What do you sell?
  2. Who is the buyer?
  3. Where are leads stuck?
  4. Optional: share CTA, post, page, or DM script.
3 · SCORE

0–12 audit

Score buyer clarity, pain urgency, credible promise, offer output, CTA clarity, and follow-up path from 0–2 each.

Safe first public reply

Thanks — I’ll keep this as a quick offer-path audit first.

What do you sell, who is the buyer, and where is the route leaking most right now: offer clarity, proof, CTA, DMs, pricing, landing page, or follow-up?

No payment step here — just the first bottleneck and next safest fix.
0–4

Too unclear for Sprint

Give one clarity action and route to SCORE, ROUTE, KIT, BUILDER, or nurture. Do not pitch payment.

5–8

AUDIT conversation fit

Give one bottleneck, one specific fix, and one clarification if needed. Invite SPRINT only when done-with-you fit is visible.

9–12

Possible SPRINT fit

Summarize why it may fit, then run the SPRINT fit-check before any scope, pricing, PayPal, or payment language.

Response format

  1. Main leak: one bottleneck.
  2. Why it matters: one short reason.
  3. Fastest fix: one practical action.
  4. Next safe route: AUDIT follow-up, SPRINT fit-check, ROUTE, SCORE, KIT, BUILDER, PROOF, SOURCE, EVIDENCE, FILTER, PROFILE, GROWTH, or nurture.
  5. Boundary: no guaranteed sales, replies, leads, revenue, followers, rankings, recommendations, or conversion lift.

Non-negotiable boundaries

  • No payment instructions from AUDIT alone.
  • No SPRINT pitch unless the audit shows fit and the prospect wants done-with-you implementation.
  • No fake proof, inflated claims, or guaranteed outcomes.
  • No spam DMs or pressure tactics.
  • Record CRM/dashboard rows only from confirmed signals.
