Quick intake

  1. What do you sell, and at what price range?
  2. Who is the buyer or decision maker?
  3. Where do leads usually come from now?
  4. What wastes the most sales time?
  5. Are you trying to improve lead quality within 7 days, 30 days, or later?

Ready: route to a relevant offer or fit-check.

50–74: Nurture. 30–49: Clarify. 0–29: No Fit.

Buyer-fit scorecard

CategoryMaxWhat good looks like
Problem urgency15The problem is current, painful, and costly.
Buyer clarity10The buyer type and use case are specific.
Budget readiness15The buyer can realistically pay for help.
Decision authority10The person can decide or strongly influence the purchase.
Speed to purchase10There is a reason to act within days or weeks.
Strategic + fulfillment fit25The buyer matches the offer and can be served without custom chaos.
Trust/proof fit10The buyer accepts realistic proof without demanding guarantees.
Referral value5The buyer can create useful insight, referral, or testimonial potential.

Ready

Move to the relevant offer fit-check. For done-with-you implementation, use SPRINT first.

Nurture

Give one useful next step and follow up later. Do not pressure.

Clarify

Ask one focused question about pain, budget, authority, or urgency before pitching.
