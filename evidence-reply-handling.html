EVIDENCE reply handling · confirmed signals only

Reply to trust-gap interest without rushing into a pitch.

This playbook handles confirmed Buyer Evidence Map replies, comments, DMs, or site inquiries. It keeps the conversation useful, one-route-first, and qualification-led before any product recommendation, pricing, PayPal instruction, or guarantee-like language.

1. Confirm

Only act when a tool or the owner confirms the reply, comment, DM, or site inquiry. Likes, impressions, and unverified searches stay as monitoring.

2. Ask one fit question

Start with the biggest gap: profile, offer page, proof/examples, process, or follow-up path. Do not send a full pitch.

3. Route once

Recommend exactly one first route: EVIDENCE, PROOF, VISIBILITY, PROFILE, SCORE, ROUTE, KIT, SPRINT, GROWTH, SYSTEM, or FILTER.

Safe public reply

This sounds like an evidence-route issue: buyer problem, proof, process, and next step need to line up before pitching harder.

Quick fit-check: is the biggest gap your profile, offer page, proof/examples, or follow-up path?

5-question intake

  1. Who is the buyer and what problem should they understand in one sentence?
  2. What proof, example, case note, demo, or visible work exists today?
  3. What process or steps do you want buyers to trust?
  4. Where does the buyer currently see the next step: profile, post, website, DM, form, or call?
  5. What outcome are you trying to clarify without promising guaranteed results?

Non-negotiables

No payment instructions before fit, scope, no-guarantee terms, and explicit buyer intent. No guaranteed leads, revenue, rankings, AI recommendations, followers, replies, sales, or conversion lift. Add CRM/dashboard counts only from confirmed signals.
