Delivery structure

  1. Snapshot score: score 0–20 and explain it in one plain-language sentence.
  2. Top visibility leak: choose one bottleneck only.
  3. Trust-route note: explain what buyers or AI-assisted discovery may misunderstand.
  4. First fix: give one specific practical improvement.
  5. Recommended first route: pick one NOVA route only.
  6. Safe next question: ask one question; do not ask for payment.
  7. Boundary: include the no-guarantee language.

Score-band routing

0–5: SCORE or ROUTE. Too unclear for paid implementation.

6–10: PROFILE, PROOF, KIT, or VISIBILITY depending on the leak.

11–15: VISIBILITY, PROFILE, PROOF, GROWTH, or SPRINT fit-check.

16–20: SPRINT, GROWTH, or SYSTEM only after explicit buyer intent and scope clarity.
