Required inputs

  2. Who is the buyer?
  3. Where should they evaluate you now?
  4. What proof/process/credibility exists today?
  5. What should a qualified buyer do next?

Hard boundaries

Do not run this from market-listening posts, unrelated searches, or public pages. Do not count published posts, pages, or views as leads. No guaranteed leads, replies, rankings, sales, revenue, or conversion lift.

Score-to-route matrix

  • 0–5: SCORE or ROUTE first.
  • 6–10: VISIBILITY, PROFILE, PROOF, or KIT.
  • 11–15: PROFILE, PROOF, GROWTH, or SPRINT fit-check after fit/scope boundaries.
  • 16–20: GROWTH or SYSTEM only if broader architecture need is explicit.

Payment boundary

No PayPal, payment, checkout, invoice, or pricing language inside the micro-audit unless the person has already passed fit/scope/no-guarantee boundaries and explicitly asks to buy a specific product or service.
