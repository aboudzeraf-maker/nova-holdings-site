VISIBILITY Snapshot Intake

A lightweight intake, fit router, and micro-snapshot QA system for founders, consultants, agencies, and service providers who need clearer public positioning, proof, and next-step routing.

This is the controlled response system NOVA uses only after a confirmed VISIBILITY reply, comment, DM, site inquiry, or owner-confirmed inquiry.

No AI ranking promises. No guaranteed leads, replies, revenue, followers, or conversion lift. No fake proof. No payment-first language.

First response

Thanks — VISIBILITY is a quick check for whether your public offer/profile is clear enough for buyers and AI-assisted discovery. Before I recommend a route:

1. What do you sell?
2. Who is the buyer?
3. Where should a prospect currently learn about it?
4. What proof or trust signal do you have today?
5. What next step should a qualified buyer take?

No guarantees — just a clarity and trust snapshot.

1. Confirmed-signal gate

Allowed inputs

  • Tool-confirmed LinkedIn comment or DM.
  • Tool-confirmed X reply, mention, or DM.
  • Site inquiry from a connected source.
  • Owner-confirmed inquiry.

Not counted

  • Public posts with no reply.
  • Market searches about the topic.
  • Impressions without conversation.
  • Assumed LinkedIn comments when no read tool exists.

CRM rule

  • No CRM lead until the signal is confirmed.
  • No dashboard reply/lead count without evidence.
  • Monitoring rows stay monitoring-only.

2. Business context intake

Identity

  • Name or brand.
  • LinkedIn/X profile URL.
  • Website, landing page, or Start Here URL.

Offer context

  • Primary offer.
  • Target buyer.
  • Main category or market.
  • Current stage: idea, early offer, selling, scaling, or team-led.

Buyer clarity goal

  • What should buyers understand within 10 seconds?
  • Where does confusion happen now?
  • What next step should a qualified buyer take?

3. Visibility friction questions

  1. When someone lands on your profile or site, what should be obvious within 10 seconds?
  2. Where do prospects currently get confused?
  3. Is the main issue profile clarity, proof, content, offer packaging, lead quality, or growth system structure?
  4. Do you have public proof, examples, case studies, demos, or process assets?
  5. Are you trying to convert LinkedIn/X visibility into calls, inquiries, sales pages, or pipeline?

4. Fit score: 0–5

Score each confirmed VISIBILITY prospect before recommending any product-specific path.

+1

Clear offer exists.

+1

Target buyer is clear.

+1

Public asset exists for review.

+1

Truthful proof/process signal exists.

+1 boundary accepted

The prospect accepts this is a clarity and trust snapshot with no ranking, lead, revenue, follower, reply, or recommendation guarantee.

Score bands

  • 0–1: SCORE or ROUTE.
  • 2–3: PROFILE, PROOF, KIT, or BUILDER.
  • 4: VISIBILITY snapshot or SPRINT fit-check.
  • 5: SPRINT, GROWTH, or SYSTEM fit-check only if scope and buyer intent are clear.

5. Micro-snapshot QA: 0–20

Use this only after the 5-question intake has enough context. It turns confirmed interest into a useful mini-diagnostic without over-selling.

Score dimensions

  • Buyer clarity: 0–4.
  • Offer clarity: 0–4.
  • Proof/trust path: 0–4.
  • Public asset clarity: 0–4.
  • Next-step clarity: 0–4.

Output must include

  • Snapshot score.
  • One top leak.
  • One trust-route note.
  • One recommended first route.
  • One practical first fix.
  • No-guarantee boundary.

Disallowed output

  • No ranking promises.
  • No lead/revenue promises.
  • No fake proof ideas.
  • No payment instructions.
  • No full-portfolio pitch.
  • No CRM lead without confirmed signal.

6. One-route-first routing

SCORE / ROUTE

Use when the offer, buyer, or public asset is too unclear for a product-specific pitch.

PROFILE

Use when profile positioning is weak, generic, incomplete, or not tied to one buyer problem.

PROOF

Use when the offer exists but truthful credibility, examples, process notes, or proof paths are weak.

KIT

Use when the prospect needs self-guided templates, prompts, checklists, or clarity worksheets.

SPRINT / GROWTH

Use when a viable offer needs fast correction or a 30-day visibility-to-pipeline bottleneck map.

SYSTEM

Use for mature operators who need CRM, content, qualification, follow-up, reporting, and asset governance.

Micro-snapshot template

  1. Snapshot score: 0–20.
  2. Top leak: buyer, offer, proof, profile, website/page, content, CTA, follow-up, or routing.
  3. Trust-route note: what buyers or AI-assisted discovery may misunderstand.
  4. Recommended first route: exactly one next path.
  5. First fix: one no-guarantee action they can take now.
  6. Boundary: no ranking, reach, lead, revenue, conversion, follower, or AI recommendation guarantee.
