VISIBILITY Snapshot Intake

A lightweight intake and routing checklist for founders, consultants, agencies, and service providers who want their public profile, offer, proof, and next step to be clearer to buyers and AI-assisted discovery.

This is not a live form submission page. It is the controlled intake script and fit router NOVA uses after a confirmed VISIBILITY reply/comment/DM.

No AI ranking promises. No guaranteed leads. No fake proof. No payment-first language.

First response

Thanks — VISIBILITY is a quick check for whether your public offer/profile is clear enough for buyers and AI-assisted discovery. Before I recommend a route, what do you sell, who is it for, and where should a prospect currently learn about it? No guarantees — just a clarity and trust snapshot.

1. Business context intake

Identity

  • Name or brand
  • LinkedIn/X profile URL
  • Website, landing page, or Start Here URL

Offer context

  • Primary offer
  • Target buyer
  • Main category or market
  • Current growth stage

Buyer clarity goal

  • What should buyers understand faster?
  • What next step should a qualified buyer take?
  • Where does confusion happen now?

2. Visibility friction questions

  1. When someone lands on your profile or site, what should be obvious within 10 seconds?
  2. Where do prospects currently get confused?
  3. Is the main issue profile clarity, proof, content, offer packaging, lead quality, or growth system structure?
  4. Do you have public proof, examples, case studies, demos, or process assets?
  5. Are you trying to convert LinkedIn/X visibility into calls, inquiries, sales pages, or pipeline?

3. Asset readiness checklist

Public clarity

  • Clear LinkedIn/X positioning
  • Dedicated offer page
  • Buyer problem language

Trust path

  • Proof or case-study page
  • Examples, demos, or process notes
  • No inflated claims

Conversion path

  • Lead magnet or diagnostic
  • Sales call/application route
  • Follow-up sequence
  • CRM or lead tracking

4. Fit score

Score each confirmed VISIBILITY prospect from 0–5 before recommending any product-specific path.

+1

Clear offer exists.

+1

Target buyer is clear.

+1

Public asset exists for review.

+1

Truthful proof/process signal exists.

+1 boundary accepted

The prospect accepts that this is a clarity/trust snapshot with no ranking, lead, revenue, or recommendation guarantee.

Score bands

  • 0–1: SCORE or ROUTE.
  • 2–3: PROFILE, PROOF, KIT, or BUILDER.
  • 4: VISIBILITY snapshot or SPRINT fit-check.
  • 5: SPRINT, GROWTH, or SYSTEM fit-check if scope and intent are clear.

5. One-route-first routing

PROFILE

Use when profile positioning is weak, generic, or unclear.

PROOF

Use when the offer exists but credibility, examples, or process proof are missing.

KIT

Use when the prospect needs self-guided templates, prompts, or clarity worksheets.

SPRINT

Use when a viable offer needs a focused 48–72h correction after fit and scope are clear.

GROWTH

Use when public assets exist but the visibility-to-pipeline engine is not repeatable.

SYSTEM

Use for mature operators who need CRM, content, qualification, follow-up, reporting, and asset governance.

Snapshot output

  1. Visibility score: 0–20.
  2. Top clarity gap.
  3. Risk note.
  4. One recommended next route.
  5. One practical first action.
  6. No-guarantee boundary.

CRM rule
