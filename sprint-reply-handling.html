Revenue Sprint Reply Handling Playbook

A controlled response system for confirmed SPRINT replies, comments, DMs, or inquiries. It protects trust by qualifying before scope, payment language, or any product-specific pitch.

1. Confirmed-signal gate

Use only after a real signal is confirmed by tool or owner: X reply/mention, LinkedIn comment/DM, website inquiry, or direct conversation.

Do not count posts, searches, page uploads, or views as leads.

2. Public first reply

Thanks — I start with a fit-check before any payment step.

What do you sell, who is the buyer, and where is the biggest leak right now: offer clarity, proof, DMs, pricing, landing page, or follow-up?

3. Private opener

Before I send scope or payment details, I run a quick fit-check so we only recommend it when there is a real offer/conversion gap worth fixing. No guaranteed results.

Five-question SPRINT fit-check

  1. What are you selling right now?
  2. Who is the main buyer?
  3. Where is the biggest sales bottleneck?
  4. Can you test improved assets within 7 days?
  5. If it is a fit, are you comfortable with the $197 beta after the fit-check and the no-guarantee boundary?

Routing

4–5: Qualified SPRINT path after explicit buyer intent.

3: Ask one clarifying question.

0–2: Route to AUDIT, ROUTE, SCORE, KIT, BUILDER, FILTER, PROOF, PROFILE, GROWTH, or nurture.

Payment boundary

Payment instructions are allowed only after: confirmed signal, fit-check complete, score 4–5, scope understood, no-guarantee boundary accepted, explicit request to proceed, and CRM/dashboard update.

No PayPal/payment language in first public reply, first DM, unqualified threads, or any thread asking for guarantees/spam.

Objection snippets

“Is this guaranteed?” No. It builds clearer sales assets and a test path; it does not guarantee outcomes.

“How much?” The beta is $197 for qualified clients, but fit-check comes first.

“No proof yet.” Route carefully to PROOF, SOURCE, or EVIDENCE if trust is the main gap.

Follow-up limit

  • 1 public reply per confirmed signal unless they respond.
  • Initial fit-check + max 2 follow-ups over 7 days.
  • Stop on no, no consent, no fit, or no response after closeout.

CRM/dashboard rule

Record confirmed SPRINT signals in /memories/nova-sprint-response-tracker.csv. Do not add qualified leads, replies, revenue, or payment status unless confirmed by tool or owner. Public posts remain monitoring sources until a real reply/comment/DM/inquiry exists.

Guardrails: no financial transactions, no guaranteed leads/sales/revenue, no fake proof, no spam DMs, no payment before fit/scope/no-guarantee boundary and explicit buyer intent.
