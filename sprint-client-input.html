NOVA Revenue Sprint

Client Input Worksheet

Use this only after confirmed SPRINT interest and a real fit-check. It collects the inputs needed to build a clear offer path, posts, scripts, follow-up, qualifying questions, and a 7-day test plan without payment-first pressure.

Use after

Confirmed reply/comment/DM/site inquiry and likely 4–5 Sprint Fit Score.

Do not use for

Impressions, profile views, searches, cold audiences, or monitoring-source rows.

Boundary

No guaranteed leads, replies, revenue, rankings, followers, or conversion lift.

Safe handoff message

Great — before any final scope/payment step, I’ll collect the inputs needed to confirm the Sprint is deliverable and useful. Please answer the worksheet below with truthful details only. If something is unclear or missing, we’ll route to the safest next step instead of forcing the Sprint.

1. Offer snapshot

  1. What do you sell right now?
  2. What is included in the offer?
  3. Current or intended price/range?
  4. Is this already available to buy within the next 7 days?
  5. What makes this different from obvious alternatives?

2. Buyer and pain

  1. Who is the most likely buyer?
  2. What urgent problem do they already know they have?
  3. What event or frustration usually makes them look for help?
  4. Who is not a good fit?
  5. Which buyer language, phrases, or objections have you heard before?

3. Proof and source material

  1. What truthful proof can we use: process, experience, examples, testimonials, lessons, screenshots, results, or customer insight?
  2. What proof is not allowed or not available?
  3. Are there claims we must avoid?
  4. What constraints, caveats, or context should always be included?
  5. Which source links or assets can be referenced publicly?

4. Current assets and channels

  1. Share the main profile, page, or offer link to review.
  2. Which channel should the first 7-day test prioritize?
  3. What content/posts/DMs have been tried already?
  4. What is currently getting any signal, even small?
  5. What should not be used because it feels off-brand or non-compliant?

Non-negotiable guardrails

  • No PayPal/payment instructions before fit, scope, no-guarantee terms, and explicit buyer intent are clear.
  • Never invent proof, testimonials, screenshots, results, or metrics.
  • Never promise guaranteed leads, replies, sales, rankings, followers, revenue, or conversion lift.
  • Do not record revenue or paid status unless the owner or an authorized tool confirms it.
