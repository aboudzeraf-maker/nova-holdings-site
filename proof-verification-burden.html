Reduce the work a buyer must do to trust the claim.

This checklist turns vague proof into a checkable path: buyer problem, evidence object, process, source credibility, constraint, and one safe next step. It supports EVIDENCE, PROOF, VISIBILITY, PROFILE, SCORE, ROUTE, GROWTH, SPRINT, and SYSTEM routing before any payment conversation.

USE WHEN

Best fit

Use after confirmed proof, trust-gap, AI visibility, source-credibility, profile/website, or evidence-route interest. Do not use it as a payment trigger.

BOUNDARY

Safety rule

Payment language waits until fit, scope, no-guarantee boundary, and explicit buyer intent are clear. Never invent proof, metrics, testimonials, screenshots, leads, revenue, rankings, or engagement.

The 20-point checklist

A. Claim clarity

  1. One specific buyer problem.
  2. No “we help everyone” language.
  3. Outcome is understandable without guessing.
  4. No guaranteed leads, revenue, rankings, replies, followers, or conversion lift.

B. Evidence object

  1. Real proof object: example, artifact, process sample, checklist, scorecard, or delivery sample.
  2. No fake screenshots, invented metrics, or unverifiable testimonials.
  3. Context explains what the proof does and does not prove.
  4. Sensitive data is removed or anonymized safely.

C. Process visibility

  1. Visible steps, not only result claims.
  2. Inputs are clear.
  3. Constraints are clear.
  4. The buyer can see how the recommendation is chosen.

D. Source credibility

  1. Evidence lives in a stable owned asset when possible.
  2. Profile, website, offer page, and proof route tell one story.
  3. Proof can be cited or linked without requiring a private sales call.
  4. AI visibility is framed as clarity/source-readiness, not a ranking promise.

E. Next-step safety

  1. One route, not the full portfolio.
  2. First response asks one fit-check question.
  3. Payment waits until fit, scope, buyer intent, and no-guarantee terms are clear.
  4. CRM/dashboard counts only confirmed replies, comments, DMs, or site inquiries.

Score routing

0–7: high burden

Do not scale the claim. Route to the Buyer Evidence Question Bank and Evidence Route QA before public CTA or pitch.

8–14: incomplete proof

Use Buyer Evidence Map, Proof-to-Pipeline, Trust Route Checklist, or VISIBILITY intake depending on the expressed gap.

15–20: controlled validation

Use value-first education or fit-check diagnostic. Still no payment language until fit/scope/no-guarantee and explicit buyer intent are clear.

Safe first response

“Useful question. I would separate proof into buyer problem, example/process, constraint, and next step. What claim are you trying to make easier for buyers to verify?”

Non-negotiables

No fake proof. No invented metrics. No guaranteed leads, revenue, rankings, recommendations, followers, replies, or conversion lift. No PayPal/payment instruction in first response.
