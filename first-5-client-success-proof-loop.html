Use gate

Do not ask for proof, referrals, or an expansion route before the client has received useful delivery.

  • Use after confirmed SPRINT interest, fit-check, scope/no-guarantee acceptance, and delivery start or completion.
  • Never treat a live page, uploaded asset, positive comment, or proposal as verified revenue.
  • No PayPal/payment instructions inside proof, referral, or follow-up messages.

24-hour unblocker

Quick check: were you able to open and understand the Sprint assets? If one thing would block you from using them this week, send it and I’ll help identify the simplest next step.

Day-7 proof and learning request

Useful question for improving the Sprint: what changed most after the delivery — clearer offer, better CTA, easier posts, better follow-up, or something else? If one sentence is accurate, can I save it as approved feedback? You can edit it first, and I won’t use your name or business unless you explicitly allow it.

Proof permission rules

  • No fake proof: no fake testimonials, screenshots, client names, revenue claims, rankings, or metrics.
  • Permission first: no quote, screenshot, name, business detail, or outcome can be reused without explicit approval.
  • Separate proof types: delivery proof, process proof, client-use proof, and business-outcome proof must not be blended.
  • Result claims: record only as client-reported and permissioned; never imply causation or guaranteed outcomes.
  • If unclear: anonymize for internal learning or do not use publicly.
