NOVA OPS Enterprise Fit-Check Scorecard

Use this only after confirmed enterprise, OPS, SYSTEM, or revenue-operations interest. It qualifies whether a B2B prospect is ready for NOVA AI Revenue Operations System or should be routed to a safer first step.

Boundary: OPS is not payment intent. No proposal, PayPal instruction, or pricing conversation before fit, scope, no-guarantee terms, contract/invoice readiness, and explicit buyer intent.

Confirmed-signal gate

  • They reply/comment/DM OPS.
  • They ask about revenue operations, CRM routing, AI workflow, content-to-conversation, or enterprise implementation.
  • They pass SYSTEM/GROWTH and show enterprise readiness.

Do not count impressions, likes, passive views, or noisy search results as leads.

First safe response

“Thanks — OPS is for qualified B2B operators who already have an offer and need a clearer acquisition/revenue operating system. Before any proposal or payment step, I use a fit-check so we only recommend OPS when the scope is real.”

9-question fit-check

  1. What B2B offer are you selling, and what is the typical deal size?
  2. Who is the buyer segment, and what triggers them to buy now?
  3. Which channels already produce any signal?
  4. Where does the system break first: positioning, proof, content, lead quality, CRM, follow-up, sales calls, handoff, or reporting?
  5. What proof, source evidence, client examples, delivery process, or credible assets can be used truthfully?
  6. What tools or data already exist?
  7. Who can execute weekly for 30–90 days?
  8. Are they comfortable with ethical/non-spam execution and no guaranteed outcome terms?
  9. Diagnostic only, or scoped implementation pilot after diagnostic?
