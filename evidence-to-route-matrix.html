Gap → route matrix

OFFER CLARITY

KIT or SCORE

If the buyer cannot state the offer or target buyer clearly, offer structure comes before acquisition volume.

BROAD CONFUSION

ROUTE or SCORE

Use a broad router when the prospect does not know whether their issue is offer, proof, profile, lead quality, or execution.

PROOF GAP

EVIDENCE or PROOF

When claims are scattered or unverifiable, map buyer problem, proof, process, constraint, and next step first.

PROFILE LEAK

PROFILE or Profile Funnel

When attention does not become qualified conversations, diagnose bio/headline/proof/CTA/Start Here alignment.

TRUST ROUTE

Trust Route Checklist

Use when profile, website, proof, and next step disagree or create buyer hesitation.

VISIBILITY

VISIBILITY

Use when the issue is category clarity, buyer-problem language, profile trust, or AI-assisted discovery. No ranking promises.

LEAD QUALITY

FILTER

Separate ready buyers, nurture leads, clarify leads, and no-fit prospects before selling.

MESSAGING

SCRIPTS

Use when ethical DM/follow-up structure is the bottleneck. No spam automation.

FAST IMPLEMENTATION

SPRINT

Use only after fit, scope, no-guarantee boundary, and explicit buyer intent are clear.

SYSTEM GAP

GROWTH

For operators with an offer and some signal who need bottleneck map and 30-day action path.

HIGH-FIT B2B

SYSTEM

For highly qualified B2B operators who need a repeatable acquisition operating system. Contract/invoice readiness before payment.

RECURRING NEED

SIGNAL or TEMPLATES

Use when the buyer needs recurring market/content direction or reusable assets after fit is clear.

Safe response templates

PUBLIC REPLY

"Helpful signal. Before recommending a product, I’d route this through one diagnostic first: [ROUTE]. What is the main gap right now: buyer problem, proof, process, or next step?"

DM RESPONSE

"I would not jump straight to a paid offer yet. First I’d score the evidence route: buyer problem, proof, process, constraint, and next step. Send 1 sentence on what you sell and 1 proof/process asset you already have, and I can point you to one next route. No payment step before fit is clear."

IMPLEMENTATION FIT

"This looks closer to implementation fit. I’d still confirm scope first: current offer, target buyer, current proof, active channel, and desired 7-30 day outcome. If those are clear, the likely route is [SPRINT/GROWTH/SYSTEM]. No guaranteed revenue/leads; scope and boundaries first."

Boundaries

  • No PayPal or payment language in first response.
  • No financial transactions, refunds, transfers, withdrawals, or account actions.
  • No guaranteed leads, replies, revenue, sales, followers, AI rankings, AI recommendations, or conversion lift.
  • No fake screenshots, proof, testimonials, engagement, metrics, or revenue claims.
  • No unsolicited replies or spam DMs.
