Research-before-Outreach Checklist

A simple route for turning cold acquisition from noisy pitching into useful, qualified conversations — without spam, fake proof, or guaranteed-outcome claims.

Best for
Service providers, consultants, agencies, and B2B operators.
Primary route
SCORE → PROFILE/GROWTH → SPRINT only after fit.
Boundary
No payment language before fit, scope, buyer intent, and no-guarantee terms.

7-step checklist

1
Pick one buyer segment.
Avoid broad “everyone” outreach.
2
Find a visible business problem.
Offer clarity, proof, profile route, follow-up, or lead quality.
3
Check public assets first.
Profile, website, offer page, content, proof, and next step.
4
Write one useful observation.
Identify a specific leak or improvement before pitching.
5
Ask one calm question.
Confirm the problem before recommending anything.
6
Qualify the response.
Ready, Nurture, Clarify, or No Fit.
7
Route to one next step.
SCORE, PROFILE, GROWTH, or SPRINT — not the full portfolio.

Safe first-message principle

A good first message should prove relevance, not pressure.

I noticed [specific public asset or acquisition route]. One thing that may be leaking attention is [specific issue]. Are you currently trying to improve [single outcome], or is another bottleneck bigger right now?
Ready
Clear offer + clear buyer + explicit problem + wants help now → fit-check, then GROWTH or SPRINT.
Nurture
Interested but timing unclear → send SCORE or PROFILE checklist.
Clarify
Vague interest → route to SCORE or ROUTE.
No Fit
No clear problem, buyer intent, or match → thank them and do not push.

What not to do

Do not scrape or spam lists. Do not promise meetings, replies, leads, rankings, followers, revenue, or sales. Do not send payment links in a first conversation. Do not pretend to have private data.
