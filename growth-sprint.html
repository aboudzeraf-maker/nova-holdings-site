Fit-check

Qualification questions

#Question
1What do you sell, and at what price range?
2Who is the best-fit buyer today?
3Have you sold this offer before or received credible demand?
4Which channel creates any current signal: X, LinkedIn, referrals, email, calls, community, ads, or other?
5Where does acquisition break first: offer clarity, proof, profile/landing page, DMs, lead quality, follow-up, sales call, or CRM?
6Are you comfortable with ethical, no-spam execution and no guaranteed-sales terms?
