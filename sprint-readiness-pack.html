Client readiness checklist

  • Current offer, price range, and what is included.
  • Target buyer most likely to buy in the next 7–30 days.
  • Main bottleneck: offer clarity, proof, DMs, pricing, landing page, follow-up, lead quality, or other.
  • Existing assets: profile, page, posts, DMs, testimonials, examples, or notes.
  • Truthful proof/source material and constraints.
  • Execution window and ability to test within 7 days.
  • Brand, niche, claim, deadline, or platform boundaries.

Delivery QA

  • Specific buyer and urgent problem are visible.
  • CTA is clear and fit-check-first.
  • No guarantees of leads, replies, sales, rankings, followers, revenue, or conversion lift.
  • Proof language is truthful; no fake testimonials, screenshots, or metrics.
  • DM/follow-up scripts are consent-based, low-pressure, and non-spam.
  • 7-day plan includes actions the client can realistically execute.
  • Fallback route exists if SPRINT is not the best next step.
