Evidence Route QA · 04:15 UTC

Confirm the signal before routing, pitching, or counting a lead.

This checklist keeps evidence/proof/trust-gap interest qualification-first: one confirmed signal, one safest route, no fake proof, no guarantees, and no payment language before fit, scope, and explicit buyer intent.

1 · Signal gate

Only confirmed signals count.

  • Tool-confirmed X mention, reply, quote, or eligible response.
  • Tool-confirmed LinkedIn comment/DM when read tools exist.
  • Owner-confirmed social comment/DM.
  • Confirmed site inquiry or form submission.
  • Direct buyer request with context and explicit interest.

If none exists, keep the row as monitoring/source status only.

2 · Reply safety

First response rules.

  • Short, helpful, and non-spammy.
  • Recommend exactly one route.
  • No PayPal/payment instructions first.
  • No guaranteed leads, revenue, rankings, replies, followers, or conversion lift.
  • No fake proof, fabricated metrics, or implied case study.
  • Ask one fit-check question or offer one diagnostic route.

4 · CRM/dashboard QA

Protect the numbers.

  • Do not add a CRM lead without a confirmed signal.
  • Keep confirmed replies, qualified leads, and revenue at 0 unless tool/owner confirmed.
  • Public posts remain source rows until someone actually responds.
  • Dashboard status can be Monitoring or Active QA asset.
5 · Posting decision

Do not post just because the cycle fired.

  • Confirmed engagement: reply first.
  • Latest X post under 60 minutes: hold public posting.
  • LinkedIn daily post active: hold another primary post.
  • Zero reach: improve assets, routing, dashboard hygiene, and market listening.
