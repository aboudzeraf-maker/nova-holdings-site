5-question route-first intake

What is the offer and who is it for?
Where do prospects first see it: profile, post, landing page, DM, referral, or ad?
Where does the drop-off happen: no clicks, no replies, weak fit, no trust, no close, or stalled follow-up?
What proof/source can a buyer verify without you explaining everything live?
What is the safest next step you want: clearer offer, better route, proof map, follow-up system, or implementation sprint?
