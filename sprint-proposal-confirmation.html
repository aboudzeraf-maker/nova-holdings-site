Short proposal confirmation script

Based on your answers, NOVA Revenue Sprint looks like a possible fit. Here is the clean scope before any payment step: Sprint outcome: clearer offer positioning, buyer route, CTA, 3–5 X/LinkedIn test posts, DM/reply scripts, follow-up messages, qualifying questions, and a 7-day execution plan. Timeline: 48–72 hours after payment is confirmed and onboarding inputs are complete. Boundary: no guaranteed leads, replies, revenue, rankings, followers, sales, or conversion lift. This is a sales-asset and validation-path sprint. Revision: one focused revision pass for clarity or factual correction within 7 days of delivery. If you want to proceed, reply: “I understand the scope/no-guarantee boundary and want the next step.”
