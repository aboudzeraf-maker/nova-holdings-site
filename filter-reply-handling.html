FILTER reply handling playbook

A practical response system for turning confirmed FILTER replies into clean buyer-fit routing: Ready, Nurture, Clarify, or No Fit — before any product-specific pitch or payment conversation.

Use when

  • A prospect replies or comments FILTER.
  • They ask how to know whether a lead is worth pursuing.
  • They complain about no-budget, vague, slow, or poor-fit prospects.
  • You need to route them before SPRINT, GROWTH, SYSTEM, KIT, SCRIPTS, PROOF, TEMPLATES, SIGNAL, or BUILDER.

First response

Thanks — FILTER is for sorting prospects before pitching. Quick check: 1. What do you sell, and at what price range? 2. Where do leads usually come from now: X, LinkedIn, referrals, email, calls, community, paid ads, or other? 3. What usually wastes the most time: vague pain, weak budget, no authority, slow decisions, low urgency, poor follow-up, or bad fit? 4. Are you trying to improve lead quality within 7 days, 30 days, or later? I’ll use that to map the conversation into Ready, Nurture, Clarify, or No Fit — no pressure and no guaranteed-results claims.

Non-negotiables

  • Do not treat a monitoring source as a real lead until engagement is confirmed.
  • Do not send payment instructions from a FILTER reply alone.
  • Do not guarantee leads, replies, revenue, sales, followers, or conversion lift.
  • Do not pitch every NOVA product at once.
  • Use one primary route first.

Ready response

“This looks like a Ready buyer-fit problem: the lead source exists, but the qualification layer is too loose. The next step is to map buyer signals into Ready / Nurture / Clarify / No Fit before pitching.”

Nurture response

“There may be fit, but urgency or buying conditions are not clear yet. Best next step: ask one sharper qualification question before follow-up.”

Clarify / No Fit

For Clarify, ask one missing-piece question. For No Fit, close politely and avoid pressure. Keep the relationship clean.

CRM and dashboard rule

  • Update FILTER response tracking only after confirmed engagement.
  • Keep monitoring rows as monitoring until a confirmed response exists.
  • Update confirmed replies, qualified leads, and revenue only from tool-confirmed or owner-confirmed signals.

Follow-up rule

  1. If the prospect answers partially, ask one clarifying question only.
  2. Do not send repeated pitches.
  3. Stop after max two value-based follow-ups unless they re-engage.
