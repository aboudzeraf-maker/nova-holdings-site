NOVA HOLDINGS • route-first diagnostic

Landing pages do not convert because the route is unclear.

AI can draft a page. Traffic can expose it. But conversion usually depends on buyer clarity, proof, one CTA, payment-order safety, and follow-up.

Use this audit before pushing more traffic or pitching a paid offer.

No guaranteed leads, sales, revenue, rankings, replies, followers, or conversion lift. This is a diagnostic route, not a payment step.

1. Buyer

Who is the page for, specifically?

2. Problem

What urgent problem or outcome is being addressed?

3. Proof

What source, process, example, or constraint makes the claim easier to trust?

4. CTA

What is the one next step before payment language?

5. Follow-up

What happens after someone replies, clicks, or asks for details?

5-question intake

  1. What is the page selling or offering?
  2. Who is the narrow buyer segment?
  3. What specific problem or desired outcome is the page focused on?
  4. What proof, source, example, process, or constraint is visible?
  5. What is the current next step and what follow-up happens after someone responds?

Route after the score

  • 0–7: Use Start Here or Route-First Diagnostic. Do not pitch a product yet.
  • 8–12: Use AUDIT, PROFILE, SOURCE, EVIDENCE, or VISIBILITY depending on the biggest leak.
  • 13–16: Use SPRINT fit-check only if there is a real offer and implementation intent.
  • 17–20: Use GROWTH or SYSTEM only when scale/process complexity is clear and buyer intent is explicit.

Safe first response

“Thanks — I would diagnose the route before recommending a product. Quick check: who is the page for, what action should they take next, and what proof/source would make the claim easier to trust? No guarantees, no payment step first.”
