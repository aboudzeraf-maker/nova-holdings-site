Trust Route · 2026-10-03 01:45 UTC

Check the path buyers must trust before they buy.

Traffic does not fix a confused route. This checklist scores whether a buyer can move from profile to page, proof, next step, and fit-check without contradiction or pressure.

20-point checklist

01

Buyer-problem clarity

0-4: Can a buyer understand who this is for and what problem is solved within 10 seconds?

02

Profile-to-page consistency

0-4: Do profile, posts, website, and offer page use the same buyer/problem language?

03

Proof/process trust

0-4: Is there truthful proof, process, example, demo, case, or credibility signal?

04

Next-step clarity

0-4: Is there one calm next action instead of scattered CTAs?

05

Follow-up and qualification

0-4: Is there a fit-check, intake, CRM note, or response path before payment language?

ROUTE

Score bands

0-5: SCORE/ROUTE. 6-10: PROFILE/PROOF/KIT/VISIBILITY. 11-15: PROFILE/PROOF/GROWTH/SPRINT fit-check. 16-20: focused test or GROWTH/SYSTEM if scope is explicit.

First response after confirmed interest

Ask: what do you sell, who is the buyer, where should they evaluate you now, what proof exists, and what should a qualified buyer do next? Then recommend exactly one route.

Boundary

No guaranteed leads, replies, revenue, rankings, conversion lift, AI recommendations, fake proof, payment-first language, or unsolicited DMs. Add CRM/dashboard counts only after a confirmed signal.
