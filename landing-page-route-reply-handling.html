Landing Page Route Reply Handling

Use this only after a confirmed reply, comment, DM, or site inquiry about AI landing pages, offer pages, buyer clarity, proof, CTA, follow-up, or traffic that is not converting.

1. Confirm the signal

Count a lead only when a tool or the owner confirms a real reply, comment, DM, or inquiry. Market-listening posts, live page checks, and published posts are not leads.

  • X reply or mention about landing-page/offer-page route leaks.
  • LinkedIn comment/DM only if tool-confirmed or owner-confirmed.
  • Site inquiry only if a connected tool or owner confirms it.

2. Safe first reply

Good point. I’d diagnose the route before rewriting the page: buyer, proof, CTA, follow-up, and next step.

What are you selling, who is it for, and where does the page currently leak?

Keep it diagnostic. Do not pitch the full portfolio.

3. Five-question intake

  1. What is the page selling or trying to get the visitor to do?
  2. Who is the exact buyer or decision-maker?
  3. What proof is visible on or before the page?
  4. What is the primary CTA and what happens immediately after someone clicks or replies?
  5. Where is the biggest leak: unclear buyer, weak proof, vague CTA, payment-first pitch, missing follow-up, or low-quality traffic?

Route decision

0–7

Start Here or Route-First. The page is not ready for a product-specific pitch.

8–12

AUDIT, PROFILE, SOURCE, EVIDENCE, or VISIBILITY depending on the biggest leak.

13–16

SPRINT fit-check only if there is a sellable offer and implementation intent.

17–20

GROWTH or SYSTEM only after explicit buyer intent, scope clarity, and no-guarantee acceptance.

Boundaries

  • No payment instructions before fit, scope, no-guarantee boundary, and explicit buyer intent.
  • No guaranteed leads, sales, revenue, replies, followers, rankings, or conversion lift.
  • No fake proof, testimonials, screenshots, or invented metrics.
  • No unsolicited replies to market-listening posts unless platform rules and eligibility allow it.
