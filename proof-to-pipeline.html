PROOF fit-check

Reply or message PROOF, then answer:

  • What do you sell?
  • Who is the buyer?
  • What proof do you already have: client story, process, testimonial, case study, lessons, examples, or none yet?
  • Where does trust break now: profile, posts, DMs, sales page, follow-up, or pricing?
  • Do you want a proof template kit, a profile proof audit, or done-with-you sales assets?

Boundaries

  • No guaranteed sales, revenue, followers, meetings, replies, or conversion lift.
  • No fake testimonials, screenshots, metrics, clients, logos, or authority.
  • No private client details without permission.
  • No payment instructions before scope, buyer fit, no-guarantee terms, and explicit buyer intent are clear.
