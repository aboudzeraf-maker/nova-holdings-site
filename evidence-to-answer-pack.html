Core question

Can a buyer clearly connect the company to one problem, credible evidence, a realistic process, known constraints, and one safe next step?

If the answer is unclear, more claims usually increase risk instead of trust.

Not a sales page

This page does not offer checkout, guarantees, rankings, lead promises, or revenue claims. It is a validation pack until confirmed demand exists.

1. Buyer question map

Capture the questions buyers need answered before they trust the offer.

  • Buyer segment
  • Question asked
  • Risk behind the question
  • Missing evidence

2. Evidence inventory

Collect real proof only: work samples, process notes, case notes with constraints, frameworks, templates, demos, and FAQs.

3. Proof-to-process bridge

Map what the proof supports, which process created it, what limits apply, and what buyers should do next.

4. Public answer bank

Create safe answer blocks for profile, website, post, DM, proposal, and FAQ use.

5. Route recommendation

Recommend exactly one next route after scoring: VISIBILITY, PROFILE, PROOF, SCORE, ROUTE, KIT, SPRINT, GROWTH, SYSTEM, or FILTER.
