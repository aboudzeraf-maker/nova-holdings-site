SOURCE Credibility Readiness Scorecard

Score whether a claim, offer, page, profile, or content asset has enough source credibility for a buyer to trust it before you pitch, scale outreach, or ask for a decision.

claim → sourceproof → constraintverification pathone safe next step

Who it is for

Founders, consultants, agencies, and B2B operators with buyer-facing claims, scattered proof, unclear source trails, or AI-visibility credibility gaps.

What it prevents

Unsupported claims, vague proof, overpromising, premature payment language, and routing every prospect into the same product.

What it produces

A 40-point score, weakest credibility gap, strongest source, stated constraint, first fix, and exactly one recommended route.

Required inputs

1. Exact claim or offer being evaluated.
2. Buyer who needs to trust it.
3. Proof or evidence object available today.
4. Source location: website, profile, document, public post, case note, workflow, dashboard, or draft.
5. What the evidence does not prove.
6. Safest next step for the buyer.

Score interpretation

0–15 Fragile source route. Do not pitch. Build the Source Credibility Ledger first.
16–25 Partial credibility. Reduce buyer verification burden before selling.
26–33 Usable but needs cleanup. Use Buyer Evidence Map or Source Micro-Audit.
34–40 Ready for a controlled next step after fit, scope, and no-guarantee boundaries.

Safe first response

If confirmed SOURCE interest appears, use this before product-specific routing:

Good question — I would score the claim before recommending a product. Send the claim, offer, profile, or page you want checked. I’ll map the strongest source, weakest credibility gap, what the proof does not prove, and the safest next asset to build.

No pricing. No PayPal instruction. No guarantee. No broad portfolio dump.

Recommended route logic

Missing source mapping: Source Credibility Ledger. Hard-to-verify proof: Proof Verification Burden Checklist. Evidence selection: Buyer Evidence Map. AI/buyer discovery clarity: VISIBILITY Intake. Profile/website/proof/CTA conflict: Trust Route Checklist. Broad growth problem: SCORE or ROUTE. Offer wording: KIT or SPRINT after fit-check. Implementation system: GROWTH or SYSTEM after scope readiness.

Boundary

Never create fake proof, fake testimonials, fabricated screenshots, or invented metrics. Never promise guaranteed leads, replies, revenue, rankings, AI recommendations, followers, sales, or conversion lift.

Payment instructions only after confirmed buyer intent, product-specific fit-check, clear scope, no-guarantee terms, and explicit request to proceed.
