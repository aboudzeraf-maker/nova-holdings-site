An ethical target-to-conversation pipeline for creating the first 1–5 qualified NOVA Revenue Sprint buyer conversations. It separates targets from leads, turns public/warm signals into value-first interactions, and routes confirmed replies into fit-checks without spam, fake proof, pressure, or guaranteed-result claims.

Use gate

Use this only to create or route qualified conversations. Do not use it for spam, bulk cold DMs, fake personalization, scraped-list blasting, or revenue claims.

  • A target becomes a lead only after a confirmed reply, comment, DM, site inquiry, referral, or explicit request.
  • Views, likes, page uploads, URL checks, impressions, and uncontacted profiles are not leads.
  • Payment instructions stay behind fit, scope clarity, no-guarantee acceptance, and explicit buyer intent.

Best-fit buyer profile

  • Freelancer, consultant, coach, small agency, expert, service founder, or digital product creator.
  • Has a real offer, service, skill, or product that can be sold now.
  • Shows a visible leak in offer clarity, CTA, proof, landing page, DMs, follow-up, pricing, or lead quality.
  • Can test improved messaging/assets within 7 days.
  • Accepts ethical execution and no guaranteed-result boundaries.

Daily 30-minute activation workflow

  1. Check confirmed inbound first; real replies outrank new outreach.
  2. Build a 5–10 target pool from public/warm-fit sources and label them targets only.
  3. Score each fit hypothesis from 0–10 across offer, buyer, bottleneck, channel fit, and readiness.
  4. Select the top 3 and write one useful observation plus one low-pressure question.
  5. Engage value-first through public replies or permitted/warm channels; do not blast DMs.
  6. Wait for confirmation. No reply means no lead and no chase.
  7. Route confirmed replies into SCORE, ROUTE, AUDIT, or SPRINT fit-check.
  8. Update the pipeline tracker with verified values only.

Safe first messages

Public value-first reply

One possible leak: the offer mentions [topic], but the next step is not obvious from [profile/page/post]. A clearer buyer + one CTA may help people know whether to reply, book, or ignore. Are you trying to fix offer clarity, proof, or follow-up first?

Warm/permissioned direct opener

I noticed [specific public observation]. This may be an offer-route issue more than a traffic issue. If useful, I can ask one diagnostic question and point you toward the right fix — no pitch and no guaranteed-results claim.

If they ask about SPRINT

SPRINT may fit if you already have something people can buy and can test improved assets within 7 days. Before any payment step, I’d run the fit-check first: offer, buyer, bottleneck, test window, and no-guarantee acceptance.

Commercial safety boundary

  • No financial transactions, withdrawals, refunds, or payment execution.
  • No guaranteed leads, replies, followers, rankings, conversion lift, revenue, ROI, income, or AI recommendations.
  • No fake testimonials, screenshots, client names, metrics, urgency, personalization, or scarcity.
  • Live page availability is not a lead, inquiry, customer, proof item, referral, or revenue signal.
