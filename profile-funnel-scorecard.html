Score the profile before pushing more traffic.

A profile is a small client funnel: buyer, problem, proof, offer, content, and next step should all point in one direction. Use this scorecard to find the leak before recommending PROFILE, SCORE, GROWTH, SPRINT, or any paid route.

24-point scorecard

01

Buyer clarity

Can a qualified visitor immediately tell who this is for?

02

Problem clarity

Can the visitor connect the profile to one urgent business problem?

03

Offer path

Does the profile explain what help is available without pushing too soon?

04

Proof/process trust

Is there truthful proof, process, example, or credibility without fake claims?

05

Content alignment

Do recent posts/comments reinforce the same buyer problem and route?

06

Next-step clarity

Is there one calm CTA or Start Here path?

First fit-check

  1. Which profile matters most right now: X or LinkedIn?
  2. Who is the buyer you want to attract?
  3. What problem should they immediately connect you with?
  4. What should they do next: comment, DM, book, join list, download, or ask for a resource?
  5. What truthful proof or process can the profile show?
  6. Where does it leak now: bio/headline, banner, pinned post, proof, content alignment, link path, or CTA?

After this, recommend one route only: Profile Kit, PROFILE audit, SCORE, Research-before-Outreach, GROWTH, SPRINT, or SYSTEM if high-fit and explicit.

Boundary

No fake proof, no guaranteed followers, leads, replies, qualified conversations, sales, revenue, ranking, or conversion lift. No payment instructions before fit, scope, delivery limits, buyer intent, and no-guarantee terms. Do not claim any X/LinkedIn profile edits happened without tool or owner confirmation.
