X post candidate

Most service providers do not need more leads first. They need to know which replies are ready, nurture, clarify, or no-fit. NOVA Buyer Fit Filter Kit is a simple scorecard for lead quality before pitching. Reply FILTER for the map. No hype. No guarantees.

First FILTER response

  1. What do you sell, and at what price range?
  2. Where do leads usually come from now?
  3. What usually wastes time: weak fit, no budget, vague pain, slow decisions, bad follow-up, or something else?
  4. Are you trying to improve lead quality within 7 days, 30 days, or later?

Use the answers to route the person to Ready, Nurture, Clarify, or No Fit.
