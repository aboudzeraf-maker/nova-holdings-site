SCORE Reply Handling Playbook

A controlled, qualification-first system for turning broad X/LinkedIn SCORE interest into one useful next step before any pitch, pricing, or payment language.

First response

Thanks — SCORE is the quickest way to avoid guessing.

Send short answers to these 5 questions:

  1. What are you trying to sell?
  2. Who is the buyer?
  3. Where does growth feel stuck first: offer, proof, DMs, lead quality, content, follow-up, visuals, or implementation?
  4. Do you already have people seeing the offer: yes/no?
  5. Are you trying to fix this within 7 days, 30 days, or later?

I’ll route you to one next step before any payment conversation. No hype, no guaranteed-results claims.

0–3

Clarify first. Route to AUDIT, ROUTE, KIT, BUILDER, or nurture. Do not pitch payment.

4–6

Build the missing piece. Route to KIT, SCRIPTS, PROOF, FILTER, VISUAL, ROUTE, or TEMPLATES.

7–10

Focused improvement or strong buyer signal. Use one product-specific fit-check before SPRINT, GROWTH, SYSTEM, SIGNAL, or TEMPLATES.

Route matrix

Offer unclear → KIT, BUILDER, AUDIT, or ROUTE.
Proof/trust weak → PROOF or PROFILE.
DMs/follow-up weak → SCRIPTS or SPRINT fit-check.
Lead quality weak → FILTER.
Content/execution structure weak → TEMPLATES or SIGNAL.
Visual launch weak but offer/buyer clear → VISUAL.
Has offer and some signal but needs a bottleneck map → GROWTH.
Needs a broader acquisition operating system and is highly qualified → SYSTEM fit-check.
Wants fast done-with-you offer/CTA/scripts/posts/follow-up work → SPRINT fit-check.

CRM and dashboard rules

  • A SCORE post is only a monitoring source until a reply/comment/DM/site inquiry is confirmed.
  • Add a real CRM lead only after confirmed inbound interest.
  • Use source tracking for public posts; do not invent replies, leads, or revenue.
  • Never record revenue unless tool-confirmed or owner-confirmed.

Safety boundaries

  • No guaranteed revenue, leads, replies, sales, followers, or conversion lift.
  • No payment instructions from SCORE alone.
  • No pressure tactics or spam follow-up.
  • Do not pitch every NOVA product at once. Pick one route first.
  • Move to payment language only after product-specific fit-check, scope, no-guarantee terms, and explicit buyer intent.

Follow-up rule

If a confirmed SCORE prospect receives the 5-question prompt and does not answer, wait 24–48 hours before one helpful reminder. Send at most two follow-ups total. If no reply, mark Nurture or No Active Follow-Up and stop direct outreach unless they re-engage.
