The 20-point snapshot

The snapshot scores five areas. Each item is worth one point. The goal is to identify the clearest next bottleneck before pitching a product, service, or payment step.

1. Buyer clarity

  • Target buyer is named.
  • Pain/problem is specific.
  • Offer outcome is realistic.
  • CTA is visible and low-friction.

2. Proof and trust

  • Clear process explained.
  • Real proof or proof-building plan exists.
  • No fake screenshots or inflated claims.
  • Risks and boundaries are stated.

3. AI-readable public assets

  • Profile/headline explains the business.
  • Website or Start Here route is clear.
  • Product pages avoid vague promises.
  • Keywords match buyer problems.

4. Conversion path

  • Visitor knows the first step.
  • Broad interest routes to SCORE/ROUTE.
  • Product interest gets a fit-check.
  • Payment waits for fit and scope.

5. Follow-up readiness

  • Lead source is trackable.
  • CRM separates sources from leads.
  • First response asks for context.
  • Follow-up is respectful and capped.

Portfolio route

  • PROFILE for profile clarity.
  • PROOF for credibility gaps.
  • KIT/SPRINT for offer clarity.
  • GROWTH/SYSTEM after stronger fit.
