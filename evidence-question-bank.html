25 buyer evidence questions

Buyer problem clarity
  1. What specific buyer problem do we want to be trusted for?
  2. Which buyer segment experiences this problem most clearly?
  3. What visible trigger tells us the problem is active now?
  4. What false solution does the buyer usually try first?
  5. What cost, delay, risk, or confusion happens if the problem stays unsolved?
Proof and credibility
  1. What real proof can be shown without exaggeration?
  2. What process example proves we know how to diagnose the issue?
  3. What asset, checklist, scorecard, or audit shows our method?
  4. What limitation or boundary should be stated to keep trust high?
  5. What claim should be removed because it cannot be verified?
Process and delivery confidence
  1. What happens in the first step after someone shows interest?
  2. What questions do we ask before recommending a route?
  3. What does the buyer receive if they are a fit?
  4. What does the buyer not receive?
  5. What timeline can be stated safely without guaranteeing outcomes?
Constraint and risk reduction
  1. What must be true before payment language is appropriate?
  2. What would make this prospect no-fit or not-yet-fit?
  3. What proof gap should be fixed before selling?
  4. What legal, reputation, or platform-risk boundary must be protected?
  5. What expectation could create disappointment if not clarified early?
Next-step clarity
  1. What one route best matches the evidence gap?
  2. What one question should be asked publicly or in the first reply?
  3. What asset should be sent only after confirmed interest?
  4. What CRM/dashboard fields need updating after confirmation?
  5. What follow-up is allowed, and when should follow-up stop?
Tracking rule

Public posts and owned pages are monitoring sources only. Add CRM rows only for tool-confirmed or owner-confirmed replies, comments, DMs, or site inquiries.

Safe answer templates

Public short reply

Good point. The useful first step is to check whether the buyer problem, proof, process, and next step all point to the same route. If you want, I can map it with a short evidence checklist.

First fit-check question

Which part is weakest right now: buyer problem clarity, proof, process, profile/website route, or next-step CTA?

Diagnostic response

Based on what you shared, the first gap looks like [gap]. The safest next route is [one route] because [reason]. I would fix [one asset] before discussing any sprint or paid implementation.

Qualified route response

This may fit [route] if scope is clear. Before any payment conversation, I would confirm buyer, current offer, proof assets, delivery boundary, timeline, and no-guarantee terms.

Route matrix

SCORE for vague growth leaks · ROUTE for portfolio confusion · VISIBILITY for AI/buyer discovery clarity · PROFILE for profile leaks · PROOF for credibility gaps · FILTER for lead quality · GROWTH for bottleneck maps · SPRINT for fast implementation after fit · SYSTEM for high-fit B2B acquisition systems after scope and contract readiness.

Boundary

Do not invent proof. Do not promise leads, replies, revenue, followers, conversion lift, AI rankings, or AI recommendations. Do not send payment language before fit, scope, no-guarantee boundaries, and explicit buyer intent.
