What should they do next? 5. What truthful proof/process can the profile show? 6. Where does it leak now: bio, proof, content, link, or CTA? No guaranteed leads or conversion lift.

Use when they want a structured profile audit after buyer/problem clarity is sufficient.

SCORE

Use when the buyer, problem, offer, or next step is still broad or unclear.

Use only after fit, scope, explicit buyer intent, and no-guarantee boundaries are clear.

Safety boundaries

  • No fake proof, fake testimonials, fake screenshots, fake followers, fake metrics, or fake profile edits.
  • No guaranteed followers, impressions, replies, leads, sales, revenue, rankings, or conversion lift.
  • No payment instructions before fit, scope, delivery boundaries, buyer intent, and no-guarantee terms.
  • Do not claim profile edits happened unless platform tools or the owner confirm them.
