Reply Handling Playbook

A qualification-first system for confirmed source-credibility, proof, evidence, trust-gap, and AI visibility interest. It separates claim from evidence before any pitch, pricing, PayPal instruction, or guarantee-like language.

1. Confirm signal

Only tool-confirmed or owner-confirmed replies, DMs, comments, or site inquiries count.

2. Map credibility

Identify the claim, evidence object, source type, verification path, constraint, and one safe route.

3. Delay selling

No product-specific pitch, pricing, or payment language until fit, scope, and explicit intent are clear.

Confirmed-signal gate

Use this playbook only when at least one signal is confirmed:

  • X mention, reply, or comment returned by connected X tools.
  • LinkedIn comment or DM confirmed by an available tool or owner confirmation.
  • Site inquiry confirmed by a tool or owner.
  • The owner provides the exact inbound message.

Monitoring only: posts, impressions, owned pages, market-listening results, and assumed visitors are not leads.

First public reply

Good question — I’d separate the claim from the source first.

Quick check: what exact claim needs to be trusted, and what proof object can a buyer inspect without guessing?

Never include: payment links, guaranteed outcomes, fake proof, or a full product-portfolio dump in the first reply.

5-question intake

  1. What exact claim do you want the buyer to trust?
  2. What source or proof object exists today: page, case note, demo, screenshot, workflow, result, testimonial, or public asset?
  3. Can the buyer inspect that source without needing extra explanation?
  4. What important constraint should be stated so the claim does not overpromise?
  5. What safe next step should the buyer take after seeing the proof?
