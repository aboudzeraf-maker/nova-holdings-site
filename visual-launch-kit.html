Allowed proof

  • Real testimonials with permission
  • Founder/operator experience
  • Process visuals
  • Product/service walkthroughs
  • Honest case examples
  • Educational authority
  • Authentic screenshots when permission-safe

Never create

  • Fake testimonials
  • Fake client results
  • Fake revenue dashboards
  • Fake screenshots
  • Misleading before/after visuals
  • Income guarantees
  • Guaranteed leads, sales, followers, or revenue

Boundary statement

The Visual Launch Kit improves presentation, clarity, and campaign readiness. It does not guarantee leads, sales, revenue, followers, or platform performance.

Routing rule

VISUAL is not the first move when positioning is weak. Route unclear prospects to AUDIT for diagnosis, ROUTE for path selection, KIT/BUILDER for offer structure, or SPRINT for done-with-you offer/message assets before visual execution.
