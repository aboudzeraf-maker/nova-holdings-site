2-minute fit-check

Answer these before any payment step.

1. What are you selling right now?

Name the service, digital product, productized offer, or consulting package. If nothing is sellable yet, start with AUDIT, KIT, BUILDER, or ROUTE first.

2. Who is the main buyer?

Describe one buyer segment clearly enough that the message can be written for them, not for everyone.

3. Where is the biggest bottleneck?

Choose the first leak: unclear offer, weak content, low-quality leads, DMs, pricing, proof, landing page, or follow-up.

4. Can you test improved assets within 7 days?

The Sprint is useful only if you can actually apply the assets quickly after delivery.

5. If it is a fit, are you comfortable with the $197 beta and no-guarantee boundary?

This confirms readiness before payment instructions. It is not a guarantee of results.

Reply script

If someone says SPRINT

Thanks — I start with a quick fit-check before payment. What do you sell, who is the buyer, and where is the biggest leak right now: offer clarity, proof, DMs, pricing, landing page, or follow-up?

Boundary: do not send PayPal instructions from a first public reply. Fit, scope, no-guarantee terms, and explicit buyer intent come first.
