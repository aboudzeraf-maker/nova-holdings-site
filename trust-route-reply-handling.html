DM/comment intake reply

Thanks — I’ll treat this as a trust-route diagnostic, not a generic growth promise.

Send 5 details:
1. What do you sell?
2. Who is the buyer?
3. Where should they evaluate you now: profile, website, offer page, deck, or call link?
4. What proof/process/credibility exists today?
5. What should a qualified buyer do next?

I’ll score the route and recommend one next step. No fake proof, no spam, no guaranteed leads, revenue, rankings, or conversion lift.

Trust Route Snapshot
Score: [0-20]
Main leak: [buyer clarity / profile-page mismatch / proof gap / next-step confusion / no fit-check]
Why it matters: [one sentence]
First route: [one route only]

Boundary: no guaranteed leads, replies, revenue, rankings, or conversion lift.
Next question: [one question only]

Score-to-route matrix

  • 0-5: SCORE or ROUTE first.
  • 6-10: PROFILE, PROOF, KIT, or VISIBILITY depending on leak.
  • 11-15: PROFILE, PROOF, GROWTH, or SPRINT fit-check.
  • 16-20: GROWTH or SYSTEM only if broader architecture need is explicit.

Hard boundaries

Do not treat market-listening posts, searches, public pages, or published posts as leads. Do not send unsolicited replies. Use X replies only when the post is eligible and the reply is short, helpful, and qualification-first. LinkedIn replies remain 0 unless tool-confirmed or owner-confirmed.
