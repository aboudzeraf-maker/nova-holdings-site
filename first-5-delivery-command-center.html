Use gate

Do not create client rows from public posts, page uploads, URL checks, profile visits, search results, or vague interest.

  • Use after a confirmed SPRINT reply, comment, DM, site inquiry, or buyer conversation.
  • Use after Sprint Fit Check answers show likely fit, or after scope/no-guarantee acceptance.
  • Payment can only be marked confirmed when the owner or an authorized tool confirms it.

Daily 10-minute operator checklist

  1. Check whether any SPRINT signal is confirmed by tool or owner.
  2. For each active slot, choose one next safe action only.
  3. Verify no payment instruction appears before fit, scope, no-guarantee terms, and explicit buyer intent.
  4. Verify no proof/referral ask happens before useful delivery.
  5. Update only verified data in the delivery/proof trackers.
  6. If blocked, classify the blocker and execute the safest fallback.

Safe stage messages

Fit-check complete → scope preparation

Based on your answers, this may fit the NOVA Revenue Sprint. Before any payment step, I’ll summarize the scope, delivery window, included assets, revision boundary, and no-guarantee terms so expectations are clear.

Scope accepted → onboarding input

Good — next I need the inputs that make the Sprint accurate: your current offer, buyer, bottleneck, proof/source material, existing assets, constraints, and the channel you can test within 7 days.

Delivery repair needed

Thanks for the correction. I won’t treat this as proof or success. I’ll fix the delivery gap first and keep the next step focused on making the asset accurate and usable.

Commercial safety boundary

  • No financial transactions, withdrawals, refunds, or payment execution.
  • No guaranteed leads, replies, followers, rankings, conversion lift, revenue, ROI, or AI recommendations.
  • No fake testimonials, screenshots, client names, metrics, urgency, or scarcity.
  • Live page availability is not a lead, inquiry, customer, proof item, referral, or revenue signal.

Next operating rule

If there are no confirmed Revenue Sprint clients, do not overbuild the dashboard. Improve acquisition and reply routing. If there is one confirmed client, move that slot one stage forward safely. If there are 2–5 active clients, protect delivery quality, follow-up timing, proof permission, and repair handling before adding volume.
