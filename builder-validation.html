NOVA HOLDINGS • BUILDER validation pack v0.1

From rough idea to testable offer route.

NOVA AI Offer Builder is a prototype workflow for turning a rough service or product idea into a clearer offer draft, CTA, fit-check, and 7-day validation path.

buyer → pain → proof → CTA → fit-check → 7-day path

Boundary: this is a validation workflow, not a live SaaS claim, not payment processing, and not a guarantee of leads, replies, followers, sales, or revenue.

BUILDER first response

  1. What are you trying to turn into an offer?
  2. Who is the buyer?
  3. What output helps first: offer clarity, pricing, CTA, posts, DMs, proof, or validation plan?
  4. Do you want self-serve guidance, AI-assisted draft structure, or done-with-you Sprint support?
  5. Are you testing this within 7–30 days?

Fit score

  • +1 real service/product/skill/audience
  • +1 buyer or niche is clear
  • +1 wants offer/pricing/CTA/scripts/proof/validation
  • +1 can test within 7–30 days
  • +1 accepts AI output as a draft to review

4–5: qualified BUILDER beta signal. 2–3: clarify. 0–1: nurture.

Safe routing

  • KIT for offer/pricing worksheets.
  • SCRIPTS for DMs and follow-up.
  • FILTER for lead quality.
  • PROOF for trust assets.
  • SIGNAL/TEMPLATES for recurring direction.
  • SPRINT only after fit-check and explicit implementation intent.

No-go rules

  • No live SaaS claim until a real deployed product/tool and onboarding path are confirmed.
  • No guaranteed revenue, sales, replies, followers, or lead promises.
  • No payment instructions before fit, scope, data boundary, no-guarantee terms, and buyer intent are clear.
  • No scraped-list spam, fake personalization, fake proof, or fake testimonials.
