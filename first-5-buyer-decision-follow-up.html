Use gate

Decision path

A · Fit pending
Prospect asked but has not answered.
Send one useful reminder; close the loop by Day 5–7 if no answer.
B · Clarify
Fit score is possible but unclear.
Ask exactly one critical question; do not pitch every route.
C · Scope pending
Likely qualified but no explicit scope/no-guarantee acceptance.
Summarize scope and ask for clear acceptance.
D · Next step
Scope accepted and next step requested.
Payment/onboarding instructions may be provided from approved route; agent does not process payment.
E · Unconfirmed
Payment instructions sent but no confirmation.
One gentle check; close if no response.
F · Input needed
Payment confirmed by owner/tool but onboarding incomplete.
Collect delivery inputs before the 48–72h delivery clock starts.

Core follow-up messages

Fit pending: Quick follow-up — I still need the fit-check before any payment step so we do not push a Sprint that is not useful.
Clarify: This may fit, but one detail is still unclear before I can recommend SPRINT safely: [specific missing detail].
Scope: If that scope and no-guarantee boundary are acceptable, reply: “I accept the scope/no-guarantee boundary and want the next step.”
Payment unconfirmed: Do you still want to move forward with the Revenue Sprint, or should I close this out for now? Either answer is fine.
Onboarding incomplete: Payment is confirmed. To start the 48–72h delivery window accurately, I still need the onboarding inputs.

Stop rules

  • Stop immediately on no, not now, stop, not interested, unsubscribe, or equivalent.
  • Stop or route away if buyer rejects the no-guarantee boundary.
  • After two unanswered decision follow-ups, move to Nurture — No Active Follow-Up.
  • Do not mark payment or revenue unless owner/tool confirms it.
